AeroChat is the messenger that works when nothing else does. No internet, no Wi-Fi, no account, no phone number. Pick a nickname, turn on Bluetooth and start chatting with the people around you, even in airplane mode.

Your messages travel phone to phone over a Bluetooth Low Energy mesh. If the person you are writing to is out of range, nearby phones relay the message (up to 7 hops) or hold it and deliver it later, when you are close again. Everything is end-to-end encrypted with X25519, Ed25519 and ChaCha20-Poly1305. There are no servers: nothing is stored or seen by us.

WHERE IT SHINES
✈️ On planes, from boarding to landing
🚢 Cruises, trains, ferries and buses
🎪 Festivals and stadiums where the network collapses
⛰️ Mountains, campsites and remote beaches
🔦 Blackouts and other times the grid is down

FLIGHT CABIN
Scan your boarding pass barcode (or type the flight number and date) to join a private, encrypted chat room with everyone on your flight. Only the airline, flight number and date are read, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

PUBLIC CABIN
An anonymous public board for passengers in range that resets every 12 hours. Simple rules, report and block, and a language filter keep it friendly.

CHAT YOUR WAY
💬 1-to-1 chats and travel groups: pair by QR code or invite link
✅ Read receipts and profile photos
📷 Photos, voice notes and voice calls between nearby phones
🎮 Mini games: Tic-tac-toe, Battleship, Scopa, Scopone and Briscola
🧠 Cabin Quiz: live trivia for the whole cabin, with a leaderboard
📡 Proximity radar: find your friend's row by Bluetooth signal strength
🌍 Offline translation of received messages (iOS 18 or later, with Apple Translation languages downloaded)
✨ Apple Intelligence on device (iOS 26 or later, compatible devices): a "what did I miss" summary of the Public Cabin and quick-reply suggestions. Everything runs on your iPhone.

STAY SAFE TOGETHER
Emergency broadcast sends "I'm OK", "I need help" or "All clear", with your GPS position if you choose, to every phone in range. Recipients get a priority alert. Handy at festivals and during blackouts: it helps you reach the people nearby, but it does not replace emergency numbers.

MORE

FREE FOREVER
1-to-1 text, Public Cabin, Flight Cabin text and store-and-forward delivery are free. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

One-time purchases, no subscription: a 24-hour Single Flight Pass, a 72-hour Travel Pass or a Lifetime Pass. One Pass unlocks photos, voice notes, calls and games for your whole group. One person pays, everyone uses it, including Android friends on the free app.
