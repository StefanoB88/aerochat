Welcome aboard! This is the first release of AeroChat, the messenger that works without internet.

• Offline chat over a Bluetooth Low Energy mesh: no Wi-Fi, no account, no phone number
• End-to-end encryption, multi-hop relay and store-and-forward delivery
• Flight Cabin: scan your boarding pass to join your flight's private room
• Public Cabin: anonymous 12-hour board for passengers in range
• 1-to-1 chats and travel groups via QR code or invite link
• Photos, voice notes, voice calls and mini games
• Cabin Quiz with live leaderboard
• Offline translation and on-device Apple Intelligence summaries
• Proximity radar, emergency broadcast, Flight card and Live Activity
• Works with Android friends too
